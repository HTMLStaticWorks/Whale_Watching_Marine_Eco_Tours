Humpback Migration 2026: What to Expect

By Dr. Sarah Jenkins | May 12, 2026

As the waters begin to warm, we are once again preparing for one of nature's most spectacular events: the annual humpback whale migration. This year, researchers are predicting a record-breaking number of sightings due to favorable ocean currents and abundant krill populations.

The Journey North

Traveling thousands of miles from their tropical breeding grounds to the nutrient-rich waters of the north, humpbacks are known for their surface-active behavior. Breaching, tail-slapping, and pectoral-slapping are common sights during this period.

"Witnessing a 40-ton animal launch itself completely out of the water is a reminder of the raw power and beauty of our oceans."

Our team has already spotted the first 'scouts' of the season, and we expect the main pod to arrive within the next two weeks. We recommend booking early, as weekend tours are filling up fast.
