Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

What happens if we don't see any whales?
While we have a 98% sighting rate, wildlife is unpredictable. If no whales are spotted, we offer a 'Whale Guarantee' voucher for a future tour.
Are the tours safe for young children?
Yes, our vessels are designed with safety in mind for all ages. We provide child-sized life jackets and have enclosed cabins.
Do you provide food on the boat?
We provide light snacks and water. Our VIP and private tours offer gourmet catering options.
