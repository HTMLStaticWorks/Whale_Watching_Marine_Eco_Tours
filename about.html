Our Story & Mission

Protecting the giants of the sea through education and sustainable tourism.

Founded in 2010

Our journey began with a single small boat and a passion for whale conservation. Today, we are proud to be the region's leading eco-tour provider, operating a fleet of state-of-the-art hybrid vessels.

Every tour we run is an opportunity to educate the public about the importance of marine biodiversity and the threats facing our oceans.

Conservation First

We donate 10% of all profits to local marine research initiatives.

Eco-Friendly

Low-emission engines and zero-waste policies on all our vessels.

Education

Onboard marine biologists provide scientific insights on every tour.

Support

Active participants in whale rescue and ocean cleaning programs.

Research

Direct funding for university-led marine mammal behavioral studies.

Our Eco-Standard

We set the benchmark for sustainable whale watching.

We provide filtered water and reusable containers for all onboard snacks to eliminate single-use plastics.

Our vessels utilize advanced solar panels to power onboard electronics and energy-efficient lighting.

We exclusively use eco-certified, biodegradable cleaning supplies to protect the delicate marine water quality.

Strict adherence to no-wake zones and international distance regulations ensures stress-free whale encounters.
