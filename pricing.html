Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you book.

What is your cancellation policy?

We offer a 100% refund for cancellations made 48 hours prior to departure. Cancellations within 48 hours are non-refundable but can be rescheduled for a fee.

What happens if the weather is bad?

If the Captain determines conditions are unsafe, we will cancel the tour. You will be offered a full refund or the option to reschedule for another day.

Are sightings guaranteed?

While we have a 98% success rate, wildlife is unpredictable. If no whales are sighted, we offer a free return voucher valid for 12 months.
